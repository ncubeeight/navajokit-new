# Training Sources

Training data have been procured from a number of resources

## Textual Passages

These sources assisted with complex grammatical syntax and provided less-common words and their more nuanced forms.

* Corley, Ron & Jean. _Corley's Navajo-English Interlinear Bible_. Bearing Precious Seed: Milford, Ohio.
* _Navajo Bible_ (trans). American Bible Society: Philadelphia (2000).

## Words and Sentences

These sources assisted with the identification of specific words and their synthesized forms, idiomatic expressions, and examples of word usage within sentences.

* Faltz, Leonard M. _The Navajo Verb: A Grammar for Students and Scholars_. University of New Mexico Press: Albuquerque (1998).
* Wall, Leon and Morgan, William. _Navajo-English Dictionary_. US Department of the Interior: Window Rock, Arizona (1958).
* Wilson, Garth A. _Conversational Navajo Dictionary_. Conversaation Navajo Publications: Blanding, Utah (1989).
* [Glosbe](https://glosbe.com/nv/en/)
* [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki)
