# Parts Of Speech

These are all the parts of speech currently trained in the _NavajoTagger_ model, which uses full word tokens.

Label | Meaning | Notes
--- | --- | ---
Adv | Adverb |
Conj | Conjunction |
Det | Determiner |
Interj | Interjection |
NObj | Noun - Object | Predicate nouns
NPostp | Noun - Postposition | Objects of postpositions
NSub | Noun - Subject |
NumC | Number - Cardinal | Will take ordinal (**NumO**) form with _góneʼ_
NumO | Number - Ordinal | 
Part | Particle | 
Postp | Postposition | May follow nouns or precede verbs
ProDem | Pronoun - Demonstrative | 
ProInt | Pronoun - Interrogative |
ProObj | Pronoun - Object | Predicate pronouns
ProPost | Pronoun - Postposition | Objects of postpositions
ProSub | Pronoun - Subject | 
Punct | Punctuation | 
Sp | Whitespace | 
VFut | Verb - Future | 
VImpf | Verb - Imperfective | Roughly equivalent to English present tense 
VIter | Verb - Iterative | Repetitive actions
VOpt | Verb - Optative | Possible actions
VPerf | Verb - Perfective | Roughly equivalent to English past tense 
VProg | Verb - Progressive | On-going actions

> Important: Commands will likely fall under **VFut** or **VOpt**. Verbs have no infinitives, but third-person singular/dual imperfective is often treated as the "default" form.
